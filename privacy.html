2. What is public on QuickSell GH

Read this section before you post anything. It is the most important part of this policy.

QuickSell GH is a public noticeboard. Once your ad is approved, the following is visible to anyone using the app:

  • Your phone number — buyers tap it to call you or open WhatsApp
  • Your display name
  • Every photo you upload
  • Your title, description, price, category, condition, and location — region, city, and any landmark you type
  • How many times your ad has been viewed

Think carefully before you make information public. Do not upload or write:

  • Your home address, or a photo that shows your house number or gate
  • Ghana Card, passport, driver's licence, or any ID document
  • Vehicle registration papers, land documents, or receipts showing your details
  • Bank account, mobile money, or card details
  • Other people's faces, without asking them first

Once an ad is live, other people can screenshot, copy, or save it. We cannot retrieve or delete copies other people have made — not when you delete the ad, and not when you delete your account.

4.2 What the app creates as you use it

  • Account identifier — a random ID for your account
  • Account status — active or suspended, and your number of ads

  • Blocked sellers — sellers you've blocked. Private to you; the blocked seller is not told.

  • View counts — how many times each ad was opened

4.3 What is collected automatically

  • Advertising ID and device data — by Google AdMob, to show ads. See section 11.
  • Technical and diagnostic data — by Google Firebase, to keep the service running, available, and secure.

What we do not collect. Being specific matters more than reassurance, so:

  • No GPS or precise location. The region and city on your ad are the ones you pick from a list yourself.
  • No contacts, call logs, or SMS. The app doesn't request those permissions.
  • No photo library access. Android's Photo Picker hands us only the specific images you select. The app cannot browse your gallery.
  • No ID documents. We never ask for your Ghana Card, passport, or licence. If anyone claiming to be QuickSell GH asks you for one, it's a scam — report it.
  • No payment data. We don't process payments, so we never see card or mobile money details.
  • No in-app chat. When you tap Call or WhatsApp, you leave the app. Whatever you say to the other person then is between you, them, and — for WhatsApp — Meta. We don't see it and can't retrieve it in a dispute.
  • No marketing emails. We only email you about your account, your listings, or a reply to something you asked.
  • No social logins. Email and password only.
  • No cookies in the app. These legal pages don't set cookies either.

9. Your rights

Under Act 843 you have the right to:

  • Be informed — this document
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate — edit your ads in the app, or email us
  • Object to processing, or withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent to publish means we take your ads down.
  • Prevent processing for direct marketing — though we don't do any
  • Delete your data — see section 10
  • Complain to the Data Protection Commission — see section 17
  • Seek compensation in court for damage caused by a breach of the Act

To exercise any of these, email stunnerconsult@gmail.com from the address on your account. We aim to respond within 14 days. These rights are free to exercise. If a request is repetitive or excessive we may explain why we can't act on it, but we'll always tell you rather than ignore you.

10. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and your data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account, then confirm.
  • By email: stunnerconsult@gmail.com, from your account's email address.

Deletion permanently removes your profile, your email address, your phone number, every ad you posted, and every photograph you uploaded. We delete — we do not deactivate or freeze. There is no recovery afterwards, so download anything you want to keep first.

What survives deletion, and why:

  • Reports filed about your listings, and the record of any suspension — kept for the periods in section 8. Without this, a suspended user could delete and re-register instantly, and the safety system would be worthless. These records hold your account identifier, not a live profile.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires.
  • Copies other people made while your ad was public — screenshots, saved images, listings copied to other sites. We have no way to reach those.

12. Security and data breaches

We take reasonable and appropriate steps to protect your data against theft, unauthorised access, cyberattack, loss, and damage:

  • Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Passwords are hashed by Google Firebase; we never see or store them
  • Server-side security rules mean you can only read and write your own data. Sellers cannot approve their own ads, promote them, give themselves a Verified badge, or edit anyone else's block list — the server refuses, regardless of what an app claims.
  • Only accounts on our moderator list can approve listings, change account status, or read reports
  • Photo uploads are restricted to image files under 5 MB and can only be written to your own folder

No system is perfectly secure, and we won't pretend otherwise. If a breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Data Protection Commission, and we will notify you directly and without undue delay. We'll tell you what happened, what data was involved, and what you should do.

13. Automated decisions

Every advertisement on QuickSell GH is reviewed by a human being, not an algorithm. We do not use AI or automated decision-making to approve ads, reject ads, or suspend accounts, and we do not send your data to any AI service.

If that ever changes, we will update this section and tell you in the app before it takes effect — and you will keep the right to ask a person to review any automated decision that affects you.

14. No limitation of our responsibility

Don't post photographs of children, or a child's name, school, or contact details, in an advertisement — not even your own child's.
