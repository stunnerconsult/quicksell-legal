16. Safety

You will be meeting and transacting with strangers. Read our Safety Tips — they're short, specific, and the advice in them is drawn from how people actually get defrauded on classifieds platforms in Ghana.

The essentials:

  • Meet in a busy public place in daylight. Never invite a stranger to your home; be cautious about going to theirs.
  • Never pay in advance — not a deposit, not "delivery", not an "inspection fee". Advance-fee fraud is the most common scam on every classifieds platform in the world.
  • Inspect the item and take possession of it before any money changes hands. Check the packed item is the one you inspected.
  • Never share your mobile money PIN, an OTP, your bank details, or ID documents. No legitimate buyer needs your OTP.
  • A price that's too good is a warning, not an opportunity.
  • We never ask you for payment to post a free ad, to release an item, or to unlock money. Anyone who does is not us.

These are suggestions, not a guarantee. Your safety is your own responsibility.
